WHO WE ARE

People-centered development with practical impact.

Rahma Social Development Organization (RSDO) is a Tanzanian non-profit organization committed to helping individuals, families and communities develop their capacity, confidence and ability to create positive social change.

Our work focuses on social education, counselling and support, youth empowerment, family support, community development and assistance to vulnerable groups.

WHAT WE DO

Our Core Programs

Our work responds to real community needs and focuses on practical, responsible and sustainable development.

Community Development

Supporting communities to identify needs, develop solutions and strengthen local capacity.

Social Education

Creating knowledge and awareness that helps individuals and communities make informed decisions.

Counselling & Support

Providing guidance and supportive counselling for individuals, families and communities.

Family Support

Promoting stronger families through social education, guidance, support and responsible engagement.

Youth Empowerment

Helping young people build knowledge, confidence, skills and positive participation in society.

Support for Vulnerable Groups

Supporting people and groups facing social and economic vulnerability with dignity and compassion.
